# FAQ

## How do I confirm that the instructions apply to my device?

Check the product model, hardware version, firmware version, and software version stated on the page. If the page does not yet state the applicable versions, contact that product's maintenance team to confirm before operating.

## What should I provide when submitting an issue?

Provide the product model and version, the host computer and runtime environment, reproduction steps, expected and actual results, complete error messages or logs, and the troubleshooting steps you have already tried. Remove passwords, tokens, and other sensitive information before sharing logs.

## How do I add product documentation?

After the product material is verified, create matching pages under `docs/zh/products/` and `docs/en/products/`, register the navigation and English titles in `mkdocs.yml`, and update both product overviews. Keep shared assets in `docs/assets/`, then run the page-coverage check and strict build before submitting.
