# Safety and maintenance

This page lists only cross-product general reminders and does not replace the safety manual of a specific product. Product-specific risks, emergency stop methods, and maintenance intervals are subject to the corresponding product documentation.

## Before operation

- Check that the device, cables, connectors, and mounting structure are intact and that the work area is free of obstacles.
- Confirm the product model, rated power supply, and the software/firmware versions currently in use.
- For the first motion test, reduce the speed or payload, and make sure the emergency stop and power-off methods are available.
- Do not perform automatic motion, firmware updates, or parameter writes before the device status is confirmed.

## During operation

- Keep people and unrelated objects away from the range of motion; do not touch moving parts while the device is in motion.
- If abnormal noise, overheating, collision, loss of control, or communication interruption occurs, stop the device according to the product-specific procedure.
- Do not bypass safety limits, and do not send unconfirmed control parameters to the device.

## Maintenance and issue records

Before maintenance, stop the device and isolate the energy source according to the product instructions. Record the maintenance date, product and version, operations performed, and problems found; if the cause of a fault is uncertain, keep the logs and contact the corresponding product support personnel.
