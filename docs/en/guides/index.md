# Before you begin

## Confirm the product and version

Before you start operating, confirm the product model, hardware version, firmware version, and the accompanying software version. Interfaces, parameters, and operating steps may differ between models or versions; do not apply settings from another product's page directly.

## Prepare the working environment

Prepare the device, power supply, cables, computer, and software according to the corresponding product page. Read that product's safety instructions before the first power-on, firmware update, or motion test.

## Follow the steps and record results

Complete the operations in the order given on the page, and record the product and software versions you used, key configurations, error messages, and logs. If you encounter a problem that the page does not cover, refer to the [issue report information checklist](../faq.md).
